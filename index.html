About Marcel

I was raised by hippie parents who took me to nudist campgrounds as a kid, and growing up I never quite understood why the human body was something to be ashamed of.

That free-spirited outlook stuck with me, though I now live in southern New Hampshire (which has terrible weather for naturists). When I’m not writing, I like to hike, travel, or watch a good movie.
